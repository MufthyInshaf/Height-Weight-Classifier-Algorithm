Why this tab exists

The original model is a plain 2-feature logistic regression, and its accuracy is unknown without data. Load your dataset to (1) measure the original model honestly on a held-out test set, (2) train an improved model (standardised features, optional BMI & interaction features, L2 regularisation), (3) find the best threshold. Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
